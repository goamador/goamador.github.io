## Learning Objectives
{: style="color: #5075ba;"}
<div class="tight-label" markdown="1">
At the end of the course, you should be able to...
</div>
* Explain the physical principles that allow microscopes to work and design and construct a compound microscope from basic components.
* Collect field samples, prepare and image their microscopic structure, and record your results in a lab notebook.
* Run image analysis pipelines that automatically detect, count and classify objects in microscopy images.
* Describe how the latest advances in microscopy allows scientists to see, and thus study, previously hidden worlds.
