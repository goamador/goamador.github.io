## Course Summary ##
{: style="color: #5075ba;" }
Have you ever wondered what lies beyond what your eyes can see? Human vision has a resolution of approximately a tenth of a millimeter; beyond that limit lies the microscopic world of cells and their intricate internal machinery. In this course, you’ll learn how microscopes work and put theory into practice to build your own. You’ll go out to the field to collect and analyze samples from water, soil, animal and plant life. Finally, you’ll learn how computers “see” images and program simple AI-powered algorithms to rapidly sort and analyze your results, teaching computers to see what your eye may miss. Along the way, you'll see how the latest advances in microscopy let scientists visualize and measure life unfolding inside living organisms. Will you join this journey into the unseen?
