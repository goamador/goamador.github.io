**Day 1: Introduction & Basics of Microscopy** <br>
*Big questions*: What is light? What is a lens? How do lenses manipulate light to magnify the very small? And how has this ability transformed our understanding of life? <br>
*Hands-on activities*: Practice using microscopes to see plant and animal life at scales invisible to the naked eye. Explore award-winning microscopy images, learn about the underlying technology and present your findings to your peers.

**Day 2: Building a Microscope** <br>
*Big questions*: How did people build the first microscopes? How have light microscopes improved since then? And how easy is it to build a basic microscope, really? <br>
*Hands-on activities*: Put theory into practice to build your own microscope in teams, compare results with professional microscopes, and understand how modern designs improve imaging quality.

**Day 3: Collecting & Imaging Samples** <br>
*Big questions*: What can you find in a drop of pond water, a pinch of soil or an unassuming leaf? How can chemical stains mark invisible structures? What are the limits of light microscopy? And how have modern microscopy techniques pushed beyond what light alone can show? <br>
*Hands-on activities*: Take a field trip to collect samples from soil, water, animal and plant life. Analyze samples in the field and in the lab. Practice staining samples to reveal new features.

**Day 4: Teaching Computers to See & Analyze Images** <br>
*Big questions*: How can images be encoded into data that computers can “see”? How do computer algorithms manipulate and “enhance” images? How can AI-powered tools learn to identify and classidy what they “see”? And how can they go wrong? <br>
*Hands-on activities*: Train your own machine learning-based image classification algorithm using real data. Evaluate its performance and test blind spots to identify what the model can and can’t do.

**Day 5: Wrap-up, Reflections & Presentation Prep** <br>
*Big question*: How do you communicate a scientific discovery with someone who wasn’t there? <br>
Student-led group discussion, reflection, preparation and rehearsal for public presentation on day 6.
